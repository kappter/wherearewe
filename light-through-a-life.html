A MUCH BIGGER NEIGHBORHOOD

Still in the Milky Way’s local patch.

The Galactic Center is about 26,000 light-years from Earth. Even at 120, light from a human lifetime has traveled less than half of one percent of that distance.

What has the pulse reached?

This is a hypothetical pulse sent from Earth at the chosen age. It is not a claim that a particular historical beam remains intact. The Sun’s light and Earth’s reflected and thermal radiation are continuous; radio transmissions are a much younger, uneven signal.

Calendar dates before the Common Era and deep-time ages are approximate. Earth’s stellar disk has no hard edge; this model uses a rough disk boundary only. For billion-year journeys, cosmic expansion means present-day distances cannot be read directly as light-travel years, so the deepest-time examples are a flat-space thought experiment.
